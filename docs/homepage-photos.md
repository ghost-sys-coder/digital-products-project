# Homepage lifestyle photos

The homepage (`app/page.tsx`) has five photo spots. Each one stays in its plain design until its file exists, then the photo appears on the next page load. No code change is needed.

## How to add them

1. Paste each prompt below into ChatGPT, one per chat message.
2. Download the image you like.
3. Save it in `public/home/` with the exact file name shown. `.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp` and `.avif` all work.
4. Refresh the homepage. Next.js resizes and compresses the images automatically.

If ChatGPT adds text, letters, a logo or a watermark, reply with: *"Regenerate it with no text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the image."*

All five prompts share the same light, colours and camera style, so the photos look like one brand shoot.

These are mood photos for the studio. When you sell a physical product, its store page must use real photos of that product, so buyers see what actually ships.

## 1. `hands-template`

Used in the "Templates you can edit" panel. Landscape.

```
Create a photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph in landscape 3:2 format, at the highest resolution available.

Scene: a close, high-angle view of a person's hands at a light oak desk, marking up a small stack of printed planning templates with a black pen. The pages show simple checklists, boxes and timelines drawn as abstract grey lines and blocks, with no readable words. To one side, an open silver laptop seen from the side, with its screen facing away from the camera. A white ceramic mug and a cobalt blue notebook sit near the edge of the frame.

Light and camera: soft morning daylight from a window on the left, gentle natural shadows. Full-frame camera, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, with the pen tip and the nearest page in sharp focus.

Colour and mood: clean whites, cool light greys and warm natural wood, with cobalt blue (#2B59FF) as the only accent colour. Calm, organised, premium and modern.

Realism: natural skin texture, believable hands with five fingers each, subtle film grain and true-to-life colour. It should look like a real photo from a professional brand shoot, not a stock photo and not a 3D render.

Do not include: any readable text, letters or numbers; logos; brand names; watermarks; visible screen content; clutter; or any second accent colour.
```

## 4. `studio-desk`

Used as the wide photo under "More doing. Less figuring it out." Landscape. It's cropped to a wide banner, so the objects need to sit in the middle of the frame.

```
Create a photorealistic editorial interior photograph in landscape 3:2 format, at the highest resolution available.

Scene: a calm, minimal studio workspace with no people. A long light oak desk stands against a white wall. On it: a closed silver laptop, a neat stack of printed pages held together with a cobalt blue clip, a white ceramic mug, a small potted plant and a simple black desk lamp. A chair is pushed in. Morning light from a large window on the left casts long, soft shadows across the wall. Keep all the objects in the middle band of the frame, with calm empty wall above and the front edge of the desk below, because the image will be cropped to a wide banner.

Light and camera: soft morning daylight, gentle long shadows. Full-frame camera, 35mm lens, straight-on at desk height, most of the scene in focus.

Colour and mood: clean whites, cool light greys and warm natural wood, with cobalt blue (#2B59FF) as the only accent colour. Quiet, orderly, premium and modern.

Realism: subtle film grain and true-to-life colour. It should look like a real photo from a professional interior shoot, not a 3D render.

Do not include: any readable text, letters or numbers; logos; brand names; watermarks; screens that are switched on; clutter; or any second accent colour.
```

## 5. `night-room`

Used behind the "Coming soon: Things for how you live" panel. Landscape. White text sits on top of it.

```
Create a photorealistic cinematic interior photograph in landscape 3:2 format, at the highest resolution available.

Scene: a calm, modern, minimalist bedroom at night, seen from the doorway. The ceiling and upper walls are washed with a soft projected galaxy: deep blue nebula clouds with gentle hints of violet and many fine, pin-sharp stars. There is a low bed with crisp white linen, a slim wooden bedside table and a small rug. The room is dark and lit only by the blue glow from above. There is no visible projector, lamp or light source, and no people. Keep the centre of the frame calm and fairly dark, so white headline text can sit over it.

Light and camera: night-time available light only. Full-frame camera, 24mm wide lens, a steady long-exposure look with very low noise.

Colour and mood: deep navy and cobalt blue, with soft violet only inside the projected nebula, and white bed linen. Quiet, cosy and cinematic.

Realism: true-to-life colour and believable room proportions. It should look like a real photo from a professional interior shoot, not a 3D render and not a fantasy illustration.

Do not include: any text, letters or numbers; logos; watermarks; people; pets; visible devices or lamps; or bright neon colours.
```
